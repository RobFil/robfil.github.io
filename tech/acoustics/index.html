Rotor position

The 5th harmonic rotates at −5ω and becomes DC in the −6ω frame. The 7th harmonic rotates at +7ω and becomes DC in the +6ω frame. Harmonic phases are relative to the fundamental current. The rotor is assumed synchronous with the fundamental: θr(t) = ω1t + δ. All amplitudes are peak values; the αβ vector angle at t = 0 is shown separately.
