TECH // INTERACTIVE LEARNING MODEL

Current Harmonics

A literature-based model of phase harmonics and their rotor-synchronous dq components.

Model convention

Phase and sequence convention. θ1 = ωet is the electrical angle, and positive phase sequence is iU,1 = I1 sin θ1, iV,1 = I1 sin(θ1 − 120°), iW,1 = I1 sin(θ1 + 120°). Thus U crosses zero rising at θ₁ = 0. The alternative pair of offsets +120°/+240° reverses phase sequence under this Clarke convention, so it would reverse the fundamental vector and the MSRF signs. Clarke is iα = 2/3(iU − iV/2 − iW/2), iβ = (iV − iW)/√3; hence iαβ,1 = I1ej(θ₁−90°) and the fundamental space vector rotates counterclockwise.

Harmonics and transforms. The balanced phase model is ip = I1sin(θ₁ + δp) + I5sin(−5θ₁ + γ5 + δp) + I7sin(7θ₁ + γ7 + δp), with δU=0, δV=−120°, δW=+120°. γ₅ and γ₇ are initial Fourier phase offsets from the signed harmonic angles −5θ₁ and +7θ₁. Define the rotor angle as θᵣ=θ₁−90°−φ₁ and Park as idq=(iα+jiβ)e−jθᵣ. Then idq,1=I1ejφ₁, idq,5=I5ej(−6θ₁+γ₅+φ₁), and idq,7=I7ej(6θ₁+γ₇+φ₁).

Meaning of dq6 and MSRF. The “dq sixth-frequency sum” plot adds the −5th and +7th d/q components in the same fundamental rotor dq frame; it is a sixth-frequency ripple in dq, not a sixth-harmonic current in the phase conductors. The separate MSRF operations are idq,5e+j6θ₁ for the −6ω frame and idq,7e−j6θ₁ for the +6ω frame, making each component DC in its own frame. Those two DC vectors belong to different coordinate systems and cannot be added directly. This app overlays their sum only in the common fundamental dq frame. It uses balanced peak currents and synchronous electrical angles; it does not model an inverter, voltage equations, saturation, or mechanical torque.

Literature basis. The signed phase-a series and the −5/+7 sequence interpretation follow Joksimovic, Levi & Vukosavic, “Near-complete suppression of harmonic currents in SPMSMs caused by back emf and dead time,” IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics, 70(5), 2023, doi:10.1109/TIE.2022.3189068 (accepted manuscript at LJMU). The harmonic synchronous-frame order and rotation convention follow Ochoa Giménez et al., “Efficient multiple-reference-frame controller for current-harmonic suppression with a shunt active power filter,” paper, especially its generic relation ωn = (s·m − 1)ω₀ and its dq−6/dq+6 examples.
