TECH // INTERACTIVE LEARNING MODEL

Current Harmonics

A technically defined PMSM current-harmonics model with a synchronous rotor reference frame.

Model convention

θ1 = ωe t and iU = I1 cos(θ1) + I5 cos(5θ1 − γ5) + I7 cos(7θ1 + γ7).

γ5 and γ7 are initial αβ-vector angles at θ1 = 0; positive angles are counter-clockwise. The 5th harmonic is negative sequence, the 7th positive sequence. The rotor is synchronous, so its d-axis has θr = θ1.

Fundamental

5th harmonic

αβ-vector angle at t = 0: 0° (0°)

7th harmonic

αβ-vector angle at t = 0: 0° (0°)

Current instant

The vertical cursor is the current rotor position and does not shift the signals. The 5th harmonic rotates at −5ω and becomes DC in the −6ω frame; the 7th rotates at +7ω and becomes DC in the +6ω frame. The model assumes synchronous rotor speed, balanced currents, and peak values. It does not model voltage limits, inverter switching, saturation, torque, or mechanics.
