The DC-link voltage is calculated from the requested d/q RMS voltages and modulation index. The vector is limited to the hexagon; this simplified model shows sampled reference voltages, not individual switching pulses. Playback is slowed down for inspection. At very high PWM-to-electrical-frequency ratios, the plots display a subset of PWM samples.
